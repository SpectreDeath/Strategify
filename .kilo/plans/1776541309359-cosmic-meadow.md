## Implementation Plan

### Phase 1: Intelligence Agent (High Priority)

**Objective**: Simulate intelligence collection and analysis (HUMINT, SIGINT, IMINT)

**Files to Create/Modify**:
- Create: `strategify/agents/intelligence.py`
- Modify: `strategify/agents/__init__.py`
- Modify: `strategify/agents/state_actor.py` (add intelligence capability)

**Components**:
1. `IntelligenceSource` enum (HUMINT, SIGINT, IMINT, OSINT)
2. `IntelligenceReport` dataclass (source, reliability, content, timestamp)
3. `IntelligenceAgent` class:
   - `collect(source, target)` - Gather intelligence
   - `analyze(report)` - Process raw intel into actionable
   - `disseminate(report, recipients)` - Share with allies
   - `counter_intelligence(target)` - Detect espionage
4. `IntelligenceNetwork` class:
   - Manage multiple intelligence sources
   - Calculate collection coverage
   - Track adversary's intel capabilities

**Test Coverage**: 85% minimum
